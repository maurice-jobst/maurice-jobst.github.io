#Questions people ask#

Who is Maurice Jobst?

Maurice Jobst is a Senior Product & Program Leader based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. He works on European payment sovereignty (EPI) at Cubic Transportation Systems and has spent 15+ years in payments, fintech, cybersecurity and public-sector mobility.

What does Maurice Jobst work on?

He integrates European Payments Initiative (EPI) digital identity with account-centric transit infrastructure at Cubic Transportation Systems, and works on AI governance for regulated delivery. His doctrine is AI at the edges, deterministic core: agents draft and triage, while anything that must be reproducible stays a script.

Which regulatory frameworks has he delivered under?

BaFin supervision and KWG for core banking, MiFID II for Tier-1 bank modernization, and DORA, KRITIS and NIS2 for operational resilience. He tracks the EU AI Act, PSD2/PSD3 and the Instant Payments Regulation for current work.

What is his most cited result?

He took a 3M+ user German public-transit platform from 1.6 to 4.0 app-store stars and carried it through the Deutschland-Ticket national rollout with zero downtime. The client renewed €2M+ a year afterwards on the back of sustained SLA compliance.

What kind of role is he open to?

Principal-level product and program mandates where payments leadership and AI governance meet regulated-industry delivery, in Frankfurt and the Rhein-Main region or remote across the EU.

Where can I see his work?

The case studies on this site, the GitHub profile at github.com/maurice-jobst, and the open-source ai-workbench repository, which publishes the research behind its own design including the two claims that failed verification.
