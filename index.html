#How I work#

Four rules that decide what I do when the answer is not obvious.

  1. 01

    AI at the edges, deterministic core

    Agents draft, triage and summarize. Anything that has to be reproducible — a converge, a deploy, a secret — stays a script. This page is the argument applied to itself: an agent wrote it, and a stdlib generator renders it.

  2. 02

    The operating model before the app

    A platform at 1.6 stars is rarely a UI problem. Fix who decides, who is accountable and what the SLA actually measures, and the release notes start writing themselves.

  3. 03

    Regulation becomes testable properties

    An obligation that cannot be tested has not been implemented, it has been promised. I take the statute apart until each clause is a property with a pass or fail, and I decide the ambiguities in writing before anyone builds against them.

  4. 04

    Claims carry their evidence

    A number on this page can be checked. In the open-source work the same rule is machine-enforced: sources cited, load-bearing claims put to refute-prompted votes, and the claims that failed published alongside the ones that passed.
